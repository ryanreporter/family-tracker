SMS & MMS Messaging Program

Consent & Opt-In Disclosure

This messaging program is private and used only by two pre-authorized household members. Each participant's phone number was added directly by the account owner, with that participant's verbal consent, to receive text messages related to household budget, food, and exercise tracking. No phone numbers are collected from the public, and no mobile information is shared or sold to third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Message frequency varies based on usage. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier plan. Reply STOP at any time to opt out of further messages, or reply HELP for assistance.
